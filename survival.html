Take the AI on a USB stick

Puts a free AI that runs with no internet, plus this whole field kit, on a USB stick or any drive. Plug it into a Windows, Mac or Linux computer later and ask it anything. Nothing to install, nothing sent anywhere.

  1. Plug in a USB stick with at least 4 GB free, on a computer with internet.
  2. Tap the button for that computer and save the file onto the stick.
  3. Open the stick and double-click the file. It downloads about 2.4 GB (10 to 40 minutes). Keep its window open until it says Done.
  4. Later, on any computer: open the "Field kit" folder on the stick and double-click the Start file. Ask opens in the browser after about 30 seconds.
  5. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway. Mac: right-click the file, choose Open, then Open.

Free and open: the llamafile runner (Apache 2.0) and the Llama 3.2 3B model (Llama community license), or Qwen 2.5 1.5B if that download fails. Needs a computer with 8 GB of memory.
